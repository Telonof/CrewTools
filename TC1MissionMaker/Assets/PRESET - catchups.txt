catchup = rubberband
These are all extremely rough descriptions of what each catchup does as the exact mechanics of the rubberband system aren't clear cut.

skill levels for bots are usually set between 0 and 1. 1 means they are highly skilled, 0 means very clumsy.
Max skill buff is 100%, max skill nerf is -100%.
